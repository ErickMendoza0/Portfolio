# Ecuador National Energy Consumption Analysis (2014-2024)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of the electrical energy demand in Ecuador over the last decade. Using a dataset of +600k records, I developed an interactive dashboard to visualize consumption patterns, regional dominance, and sector-specific trends.

## 📊 Key Insights
* **Regional Dominance:** Guayas and Pichincha account for the highest energy load, with Guayas showing a balanced mix between Industrial and Residential sectors.
* **Historical Trends:** A steady growth in demand is observed post-2021, reflecting economic recovery and industrial expansion.
* **Sector Analysis:** The Residential sector remains the primary load base nationwide, while the Industrial sector shows higher sensitivity to economic shifts.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Data Source:** National Agency for Regulation and Control of Energy (ARCONEL).
* **Tool:** Tableau Public.
* **Techniques:** Geospatial Mapping, Time-Series Analysis, Interactive Filtering, and Data Cleaning (Python).
